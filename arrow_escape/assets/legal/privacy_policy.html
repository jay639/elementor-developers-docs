3. How we use data and legal bases

  • Provide the game (save, sync, deliver purchases) – performance of our contract with you.
  • Keep it working and secure (crash reports, fraud and cheat prevention, app integrity checks) – our legitimate interests.
  • Improve the game (anonymous gameplay analytics) – legitimate interests, or consent where required. You can switch it off in Settings → Privacy → Share usage analytics.
  • Ads – non-personalised ads under legitimate interests; personalised ads only with your consent where the law requires it (see section 5).
  • Legal obligations – e.g. tax records for purchases (kept by the app stores).

We do not use your data for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Service providers (partners)

We share data only with providers that help us run the App, under contracts that limit their use of it:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Remote Config, App Check) – firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob – policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Ad networks through AdMob mediation: AppLovin (applovin.com/privacy), Unity Ads (unity.com/legal), Meta Audience Network (facebook.com/privacy/policy), ironSource / Unity LevelPlay (is.com privacy)
  • RevenueCat (purchase validation) – revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Google Play and Apple App Store (downloads, payments, refunds) – their own privacy policies apply.
  • If we run install campaigns, advertising platforms such as Meta or Google Ads may receive install and in-app events to measure them.

We may also disclose data if required by law, to protect our rights or users' safety, or as part of a merger or sale of the business (with notice to you).

5. Advertising

  • The App is free and shows rewarded video ads (only when you choose to watch one, e.g. for a hint, an extra heart or double coins), interstitial ads between some levels (never before level 10 and not more than about once every few minutes), and a banner on the city map. Buying Remove Ads removes interstitials and banners.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: we ask for your consent through Google's consent message (IAB TCF) before personalised ads. Change it any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • US states (California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon and others): personalised ads may be a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under state law. You can opt out in Settings → Privacy options where shown, or in your device settings.
  • iOS: we ask permission through App Tracking Transparency before using your IDFA. If you decline, ads are not personalised using it.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
  • Ads are filtered for a general audience (no adult, gambling or similar mature categories) and are never personalised for users we know to be children.

6. In-app purchases

  • Optional purchases: Remove Ads (one-time), coin packs and a starter bundle. Prices are shown in your local currency before you buy.
  • Payments are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive your card or bank details.
  • Remove Ads is restored automatically on Android and through Shop → Restore purchases on iOS when you use the same store account. Coins and other consumable items are used up and cannot be restored by the store.
  • Coins are a virtual item with no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money.
  • Refunds follow the store's policy: Google Play (support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637) or Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com).
  • Parents: you can require approval for purchases with Google Family Link / Play purchase authentication or Apple Screen Time and Ask to Buy.

7. Children and age

  • The App is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (US COPPA) or under the age of digital consent in their country (EU/UK GDPR Article 8: 13–16 depending on the country; India DPDP Act: 18).
  • Our store listings declare a target audience of 13+, the App does not take part in the Google Play Families programme or the Apple Kids category, and ad requests are not marked as child-directed.
  • If we learn that we hold personal data of a child below these ages without verifiable parental consent, we delete it. Parents or guardians can contact privacy@riseivo.com to request review or deletion.
  • We follow the principles of the UK Age Appropriate Design Code: privacy-friendly defaults, no profiling of known children, no nudging to weaken privacy settings.

8. How long we keep data

  • Cloud progress: while you keep playing, or until you ask us to delete it.
  • Analytics events: up to 14 months (Google Analytics retention).
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Purchase records: as long as required by tax and accounting law.
  • Ad partners keep data under their own policies (links above).

9. Security

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Saved progress on your device is encrypted with a key held in the device's secure storage. Cloud data is protected by access rules so each anonymous player can reach only their own record, and app-integrity checks (Play Integrity / App Attest) block modified apps. No method is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data.

10. International transfers

Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, transfers are protected by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK Addendum or the EU–US / UK / Swiss Data Privacy Framework, and equivalent safeguards under other laws.

11. Your choices and rights

In the app: turn off analytics (Settings → Privacy → Share usage analytics), change ad consent (Settings → Privacy options), and reset your advertising ID in your device settings. Uninstalling the App stops all collection.

By email (privacy@riseivo.com): ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, object to or restrict processing, or withdraw consent. Tell us the device and approximate date you started playing so we can find your anonymous record. We reply within 30 days (45 days under US state laws) and never discriminate against you for using your rights.

Region-specific rights

  • EU/EEA, UK, Switzerland (GDPR, UK GDPR, FADP): all rights above, plus the right to complain to your data protection authority.
  • USA (CCPA/CPRA and other state laws): right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of "sale"/"sharing" for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information for money.
  • Brazil (LGPD), Canada (PIPEDA), Australia (Privacy Act), Japan (APPI), South Korea (PIPA), Singapore (PDPA), South Africa (POPIA): rights of access, correction, deletion and complaint to your local regulator.
  • India (DPDP Act 2023): rights to information, correction, erasure, grievance redressal and nomination. Grievance contact: privacy@riseivo.com.
  • China (PIPL): if the App is offered in your region, you have rights to know, decide, access, copy, correct and delete, and cross-border transfer is done only as the law allows.

12. Deleting your data

Email privacy@riseivo.com with the subject "Delete my data". We delete your cloud progress and ask our providers to delete associated data within 30 days. Data kept by app stores for purchases and by ad partners is handled under their policies.
