## What we collect
- **Anonymous account id.** The App signs you in anonymously (Firebase
  Authentication) so your progress can be saved to the cloud. We don't ask
  for your name or email.
- **Game progress.** Levels, stars, coins, skins and settings, stored on
  your device (encrypted) and in Google Cloud Firestore under your
  anonymous id.
- **Usage and diagnostics.** Events such as levels started and completed,
  ads shown and purchases, plus crash reports (Firebase Analytics and
  Crashlytics).
- **Advertising.** Our ad partners (Google AdMob and, through mediation,
  AppLovin, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network and ironSource/LevelPlay) may
  collect your device's advertising identifier and similar data to show and
  measure ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first;
  on iOS we ask permission through App Tracking Transparency. You can
  change your choice in **Settings → Privacy options**.
- **Purchases.** Handled by Apple / Google; RevenueCat processes purchase
  receipts to unlock what you bought. We never see your payment details.

## How we use it
To save and sync your game, keep the App working and fix crashes, improve
the game's design and difficulty, show ads (personalised only with consent)
and deliver purchases.

## Children
The App is rated for everyone but is not directed at children under 13
(or the equivalent age in your country). We do not knowingly collect
personal data from children.
