## Full description

Every car wants out. Only some of them can go.

Arrow Escape is a calm, clever traffic puzzle. Each car points the way it
will drive. Tap it, and if the road ahead is clear, off it goes. If it
isn't, it bumps the car in front and you lose a heart. Clear the whole board
to escape the jam.

It's easy to learn and surprisingly deep. Spot which car has to go first,
untangle long chains of waiting traffic, and find the one order that frees
everything.

**1,500 levels with a smooth difficulty curve**
Every level is checked by a solver, so each one can always be solved. A
gentle "breather" level arrives every five levels to keep things relaxing.

**New ideas as you go**
• Trucks: long vehicles that follow their cab around corners
• Traffic lights: they switch every time a car leaves
• One-way gates: they turn traffic in the arrow's direction
• Ambulances: clear their lane first!

**A city that grows with you**
Every 10 levels builds a new block: houses, shops, towers and parks.

**Daily challenge**
A new puzzle every day, the same for everyone. It gets harder through the
week, with the toughest on Sunday. Keep your streak going and claim a daily
reward that grows each day.

**Make it yours**
Unlock vehicle skins with coins: Candy, Ocean, Forest, Sunset, Ink, Neon
and Gold.

**Thoughtfully accessible**
Light and dark themes, thick-arrow, high-contrast and colorblind modes,
reduce-motion, large tap targets and full screen-reader support.

**Fair play**
Hints, an extra heart and doubled coins are always optional videos. Remove
ads with one purchase. Your progress is saved to the cloud automatically.
