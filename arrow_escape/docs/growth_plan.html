Where the two reports agree, and where to adjust

The ChatGPT report is a sound strategy, written without access to our source code or the competitor APKs (it says so in its evidence note). Several of its "new" items already ship, and a few numbers need correcting against what we measured.

ChatGPT recommendationVerdictWhat the data and code say
Make arrows 25–35% thinnerAdjustThinning alone won't fix it. 83–99% of our arrows are one cell long; the leaders draw long winding lines. Make them long and about 60% thinner (stroke from 0.32 to ~0.13 of a cell). Keep "thick arrows" as an accessibility setting.
Increase dependency depth, not just arrow countAgreeExactly right. Long arrows are what makes depth possible: one long arrow blocks many others. Our chains are only 2–6 deep today.
Interactive tutorial: tap, pinch, blocked path, complete; skippable; trackedAgreeMatches what apps 1, 2 and 3 ship (hand, "Pinch to zoom", mechanic popups). Its suggested copy is good; use it.
Distinct tap, valid, blocked, escape, complete feedbackAgreeBump, flash, haptics and sounds exist. Add the missing pieces: tap highlight, exit trail and sparks, board-clear transition, combo praise.
Larger invisible hit area; no double input during animationAlready builtNearest-arrow tolerance of 0.75 cell, per-arrow animation lock, debounce, and swallowing taps on just-exited cells.
Build a level validation engineAlready builtSolver, analyzer (moves, initial legal moves, chain depth, blocked share, dead ends) and CI verification of all 1,500 levels. Next step: calibrate it with real player data (see analytics).

Merged priorities

P0 · Core feel (first)

  • Long, thin, rounded arrows over a soft dot grid, generated to fill the board shape.
  • Faster difficulty curve with Normal / Hard / Super Hard tiers and a breather after each Hard.
  • First-session tutorial: hand on a free arrow, blocked-path demo, pinch animation on the first big board, mechanic popups.
  • Feedback polish: tap highlight, exit trail, sparks, board-clear transition.
  • Analytics foundation (events below) before the changes ship, so we can measure the difference.

P1 · Reasons to come back

  • Push notifications (local first).
  • Combo meter and praise; encouragement at 25%, 75%, 90%.
  • Achievements; daily calendar with monthly trophy; shareable daily result.
  • Boosters: Eraser and Grid Guide added to Hint and Undo.
  • City blocks and skins tied to milestones; shape album.

P2 · Growth

  • Zen mode, time attack, weekly leaderboard for the daily.
  • One new mechanic per update; themed events.
  • Offers tested with Remote Config A/B tests against retention.

Analytics guide

Product analytics

Firebase Analytics, already in the app. Free, Google Ads and AdMob linking, audiences for push targeting.

Raw data

BigQuery export from Firebase (free tier covers us early). Needed for per-level funnels and the difficulty loop.

Experiments

Remote Config + A/B Testing, already wired for ad frequency. Use it for curve slope, hearts, tutorial variants.

Stability

Crashlytics, already in. Upload the obfuscation symbols from each release build.

Optional later: GameAnalytics (free, ready-made progression dashboards) and an attribution SDK such as AppsFlyer or Adjust only once you buy installs.

What we log today

level_start, level_complete (stars, mistakes, seconds), level_fail, ad_show, ad_reward, ad_fail, purchase_success / purchase_fail, save_tamper. Missing: quits, attempts, help used per level, tutorial steps, economy, notifications, user properties and ad revenue.

Firebase limits: event names up to 40 characters, 25 parameters per event, values up to 100 characters, 500 distinct event names. Never send names, emails or free text. Firebase logs first_open, session_start, app_remove and Play purchases by itself.

The loop that fixes difficulty for good

  1. Every level already ships with a predicted difficulty from our analyzer.
  2. BigQuery query per level: first-try win rate, median seconds, mistakes, hints, quit rate.
  3. Fit the analyzer weights so predicted difficulty matches what players experienced; flag levels far off target.
  4. Regenerate those levels (or the curve) with the tool, verify in CI, ship as a new level pack.

Privacy and consent

  • Tie analytics to the existing consent flow: Firebase consent mode (setConsent) with ad storage and personalisation off until the UMP form allows it.
  • Analytics opt-out switch in Settings; respect it everywhere.
  • Update the Play Data safety form and privacy policy (India DPDP Act 2023, GDPR, COPPA "not directed at children").
  • Use the anonymous user id only; no personal data in events.

Push notifications

Local (build first)

Scheduled on the phone with flutter_local_notifications. No server, works offline, covers most value: daily ready, streak at risk, reward waiting, comeback.

Remote (then)

firebase_messaging for campaigns and events, targeted with Analytics audiences from the Firebase console. iOS needs an APNs key.

Rules that keep players from turning them off

  • At most one per day; quiet hours 10 pm–8 am in the player's time zone.
  • Cancel and reschedule every time the app opens, so nobody gets "come back" after playing.
  • Stop a type after three ignored in a row; per-type switches in Settings.
  • Ask for permission after the first win or level 3, with a short in-app explanation first, never on first launch. Android 13+ requires the runtime prompt; we already declare POST_NOTIFICATIONS.
  • Every notification deep-links to the right screen (go_router already handles routes) and logs notification_open.
  • No ads in notifications (Play policy); localise copy.

Sprint plan

Sprint 1 · Measure, then fix the feel (about 2 weeks)

  • Analytics events, user properties, consent mode, ad revenue; BigQuery export on.
  • Long thin arrows + dot grid; new generator settings; regenerate and verify 1,500 levels.
  • Difficulty tiers and faster curve; tutorial with hand, blocked demo and pinch.
  • Ship to a closed test track; record baseline metrics for a week.

Sprint 2 · Come-back loop (about 2 weeks)

  • Local notifications with permission prompt and settings.
  • Combo and praise, trails and sparks, board-clear transition.
  • Achievements, daily calendar and trophy, shareable result, rating prompt.

Sprint 3 · Tune and grow

  • First difficulty-calibration pass from BigQuery data.
  • Remote push campaigns; A/B tests on curve, hearts and ad frequency.
  • Zen mode, boosters, music; then one new mechanic.
